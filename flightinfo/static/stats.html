A sample every 15 minutes misses an aircraft that crossed in less, so an aircraft count is a floor, never a total. Messages are what the receiver decoded. Everything on this page follows the period chosen at the top. The lists count each aircraft once in the period: airlines by callsign, models from the aircraft database, countries from the aircraft's address. An airline's flag is the country it is based in (cargo and airline lists checked against a published source), else the one its aircraft are registered in.
