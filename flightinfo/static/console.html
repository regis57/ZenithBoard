The web console is off. To turn it on, over SSH on the Pi:

sudo zenithboard console on

Or in the menu: Settings → 7 Web console. The wall.

Or sign in with your phone

No console password yet. Choose one over SSH on the Pi:

sudo zenithboard console password

Pairing this device…

Sign in a phone without typing

On a computer already signed in: System → Signed in → Pair a phone, or over SSH:

sudo zenithboard console pair

Then scan the QR code with the phone's camera. It works once, within five minutes; the phone then stays signed in as long as it opens the console once a month. No password is needed for it.

That QR code is never shown on this page, on purpose: here it would sign in anyone on your network who scans it. (With phone sign-in on, this page shows another one: it signs this computer in, once a phone already signed in says yes.)
